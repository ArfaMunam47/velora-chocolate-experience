    Product Story

    Made to be Noticed.

    Every detail of VELORA is engineered for the senses — from the weight of the bar in your hand to the way it breaks.

    Craftsmanship

    The Art Behind the Bar.

    From bean to bar, every stage is deliberate — a process refined over a decade of obsession.

    01

    Selective Sourcing

    We work directly with a handful of family farms, paying above market rate for beans harvested at peak ripeness.

    02

    Slow Conching

    Each batch is conched for 72 hours at controlled temperatures, developing aroma without bitterness.

    03

    Precision Tempering

    Hand-tempered in small batches to achieve the signature VELORA snap and mirror-glossed finish.
